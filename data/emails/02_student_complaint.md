# Email: student complaint about an OSCE result

*Fictional email, created for training. Use it for Workshop 3, Exercise 3 (reply with the right tone).*

---

**From:** Student, Year 3 (fictional)
**To:** OSCE coordinator
**Date:** Friday 6 November 2026, 23:47
**Subject:** UNFAIR RESULT!!!

Hello,

I just saw my OSCE result on the portal and I FAILED the medication station even though I did everything right. The examiner was looking at her phone half the time and didn't even watch me properly. I got 14/20 at that station, which is a good score, but it says "critical safety error" and the station is failed. Nobody ever told us about this rule!!! This is completely unfair and I want my exam re-marked immediately. Otherwise I'll go to the Dean.

I studied so hard for this and now I have to do the resit in June which ruins my summer plans.

Regards

---

**Facts the coordinator knows** (do not paste these into a public AI tool with the student's name; anonymise)

- The rule on critical patient-safety errors is in §4 of the OSCE regulations 2026–2027, published on the portal in October 2026 and presented at the information session on 15 October.
- The examiner's marking sheet notes: "did not check the patient's identity before administering the medication."
- Students can request a review of the marking procedure (not of the academic judgement) within 10 working days, using the form on the portal.
- Written station-by-station feedback will be sent within 15 working days of the exam (§9).
- The comment about the examiner using her phone must be passed on to the OSCE lead and looked into.
