# Clinical Placement Handbook 2026–2027

*St Aldric School of Health Sciences (fictional institution, created for training purposes).*
*Edition of September 2026.*

## 1. Placement duration and hours

Each clinical placement lasts 5 weeks. Students work 35 hours per week, following the shift pattern of their clinical team, including some evening and weekend shifts.

## 2. Supervision

Each student is assigned a named clinical supervisor in the placement area and an academic tutor at the school. A mid-placement review with the clinical supervisor takes place during week 3.

## 3. Reporting an absence

A student who cannot attend a shift must inform both the clinical supervisor and the Placement Office (placements@st-aldric.example) before 8:00 on the day of the absence. Absences of more than 3 days in a single placement must be made up before the placement can be validated.

## 4. Assessment of the placement

Students keep a competency logbook, which is signed by the clinical supervisor. The final placement assessment form must be uploaded to the student portal within 7 days of the end of the placement.

## 5. Needle-stick and sharps injuries

In case of a needle-stick or sharps injury: encourage the wound to bleed gently, wash it with soap and running water, do not scrub or suck the wound, and cover it with a waterproof dressing. Report the injury immediately to the person in charge of the ward and go to Occupational Health (or to the Emergency Department out of hours) without delay, because post-exposure prophylaxis is most effective when started as early as possible, ideally within 1 hour. Complete an incident report on the same day.

## 6. Confidentiality and patient data

Students must never record patient-identifiable information (name, date of birth, hospital number, photographs) in their notes, assignments or reflective logs. Patient information must never be entered into public AI tools such as chatbots. Case descriptions used for coursework must be fully anonymised.

## 7. Dress code

Students wear the school's clinical uniform and their identity badge at all times. In clinical areas they comply with the "bare below the elbows" policy: short sleeves, no wristwatch, no jewellery except a plain wedding band, short nails without nail varnish or artificial nails.

## 8. Concerns about patient safety

A student who witnesses a situation that puts patient safety at risk must raise the concern with their clinical supervisor or, if this is not possible, with their academic tutor, within 24 hours. The school guarantees that students raising concerns in good faith will not be penalised.
