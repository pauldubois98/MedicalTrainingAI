**Debrief points**

- Out of 8: 3 correct, 2 with errors, 3 fabricated. This would be a realistic result for an assistant **without** web search (see Walters & Wilder, *Sci Rep* 2023: 55% of GPT-3.5 and 18% of GPT-4 references fabricated, and 43% / 24% of the real ones with substantive errors).
- Assistants **with** web search or a literature database fabricate much less, but can still misattribute claims. Always read at least the abstract.
- Habit to build: never cite a reference you have not opened yourself.
