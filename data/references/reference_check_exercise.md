# Exercise: Can you trust this bibliography?

> **How to check**
> 1. If there is a DOI, paste it after `https://doi.org/` and see **which** article opens.
> 2. Search the exact title in quotes on [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov) or [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com).
> 3. Compare authors, year, journal, volume and pages.
