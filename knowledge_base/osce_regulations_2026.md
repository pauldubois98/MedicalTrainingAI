# OSCE Examination Regulations 2026–2027

*St Aldric School of Health Sciences (fictional institution, created for training purposes).*
*Version 3, adopted by the Examination Board on 2 October 2026.*

## 1. Scope

These regulations apply to all Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCEs) taken by Year 2 and Year 3 nursing and medical students during the 2026–2027 academic year. They replace the 2025–2026 regulations.

## 2. Exam dates

The main OSCE session takes place from 18 to 22 January 2027. The resit session takes place on 14 and 15 June 2027. Individual timetables are published on the student portal three weeks before each session.

## 3. Exam format

The OSCE consists of 10 stations. Each station lasts 8 minutes, preceded by 2 minutes of reading time outside the room. One additional rest station is included in each circuit. Stations assess history taking, clinical examination, communication, procedural skills and clinical reasoning.

## 4. Passing criteria

To pass the OSCE, a student must obtain an overall score of at least 50% and pass at least 7 of the 10 stations. A critical patient-safety error at any station (for example, failing to check patient identity before a procedure) results in that station being failed, regardless of the score obtained.

## 5. Absence on the day of the exam

A student who is absent must send a medical certificate to the Examination Office within 72 hours. A justified absence is not counted as an attempt, and the student is automatically registered for the resit session. An unjustified absence counts as a failed attempt.

## 6. Resits

Each student is allowed one resit per academic year. The resit follows the same format and passing criteria as the main session. Resit results are recorded as pass or fail only.

## 7. Reasonable adjustments

Students who need reasonable adjustments (for example, extra reading time or a quiet waiting room) must submit a request to the Disability Support Office before 1 December 2026. The standard adjustment is 1 extra minute of reading time per station.

## 8. Conduct during the exam

Mobile phones and smartwatches must be left in the lockers provided. Students must wear the school's clinical uniform and comply with the "bare below the elbows" policy. Any communication between students during the circuit is treated as misconduct.

## 9. Results and feedback

Provisional results are published on the student portal within 10 working days. Each student receives written station-by-station feedback within 15 working days of the exam.
