# Before the training: setup (15 min)

To make the most of the hands-on workshops, please create the following **free** accounts **before the day** and check that you can log in from your own laptop.

## Accounts to create

| Tool | What for | Link | Account |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A general AI assistant** (at least one) | Workshops 1 and 3 | [claude.ai](https://claude.ai), [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com), [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com), [chat.mistral.ai](https://chat.mistral.ai) or [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com) | Free |
| **NotebookLM** | Workshop 2 (knowledge base) | [notebooklm.google.com](https://notebooklm.google.com) | Free, needs a Google account |
| **Consensus** or **Elicit** | Workshop 1 (literature search) | [consensus.app](https://consensus.app), [elicit.com](https://elicit.com) | Free tier |
| **Zotero** (optional) | Workshop 1 (bibliography) | [zotero.org](https://www.zotero.org) + browser connector | Free |

<div class="tip">

**Tip:** having **two** different assistants (for example Claude and ChatGPT) is useful: you can compare their answers on the same question.

</div>

## Check that your institution allows it

Some hospitals and universities restrict or provide specific AI tools (for example an institutional Copilot or ChatGPT licence). If your institution has an approved tool, use it, and bring your login details.

## Bring along (optional, but recommended)

- **2 to 5 documents from your own teaching**: course notes, a guideline you teach, a programme handbook, an exam regulation. Use **non-confidential** documents only, in PDF or Word format.
- **A literature topic** you are interested in (for a lecture, a thesis you supervise, a research project).
- **One or two repetitive emails** you often write (with all names and personal data removed).

<div class="warn">

**Never use real patient data, or personal data about students or colleagues, during the workshops.** All the example data provided for the course is fictional.

</div>

## Course materials

All materials (slides, interactive demos, example documents) are provided by the trainer. The example documents come from the **St Aldric School of Health Sciences**, a fictional institution invented for this course.
