# Workshop 3: Designing a course with AI

<div class="key">
**Goal:** go from a topic to a structured session.
</div>

## Step 1: Learning outcomes & session plan

Define the learning outcomes of the session.
Ask a plan for a session before writing material.

## Step 2: Iterate

It is much easier to iterate at a session plan level than on a material level.
Hence iterating on the plan is more efficient than once the full material is ready.

## Step 3: Materials

- **Slide** for lectures
- **Interactive page** to understand visually
- **Practical exercises** for practice sessions
- **Quiz** to test students
Advanced/complex material does not take ore time to create. 

## Step 4: Iterate

Iterate until material is of satisfactory quality.

<div class="warn">

**You are the author.** The AI can be wrong on a dose or invent a protocol: check every fact before teaching it.

</div>
